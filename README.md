# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and organizational performance metrics.

This dashboard helps HR professionals and business leaders identify key factors influencing employee turnover and make data-driven workforce management decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To provide actionable insights into employee attrition patterns by analyzing workforce data across departments, age groups, education fields, job roles, and other HR metrics.

---

## 🚀 Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Total Attrition
- Attrition Rate (%)
- Active Employees
- Average Employee Age

### Interactive Analysis

- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Education Field
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Gender
- Job Satisfaction Analysis
- Employee Demographics Overview

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### Employee Attrition Analysis
- Identify departments with the highest turnover.
- Analyze employee attrition across different age groups.
- Understand attrition trends by job role and education background.

### Workforce Demographics
- Gender distribution analysis.
- Employee age distribution.
- Educational qualification breakdown.

### Job Satisfaction Insights
- Measure satisfaction levels across different job roles.
- Discover potential relationships between satisfaction and attrition.

- Is there a relationship between job satisfaction and employee retention?
